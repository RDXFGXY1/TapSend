Pairing and devices

QR codes, pairing passes, approval, renaming, and forgetting phones

Pair with a QR code

  1. On Windows, press P. The pairing screen shows the code and computer address.
  2. On Android, open Tools → Connect a PC. Enter a device name if you want one, then choose Scan QR.
  3. Scan the QR displayed by the computer. If Android requests approval, press V on Windows, highlight that phone, and press A.

The phone remembers a successful pairing. Press U on Windows to refresh the QR and pairing pass if they expire or the computer's address changes.

Pair without a camera

On the Windows pairing screen, press Y to copy the pairing pass. Move it privately to the phone and copy it there. In Connect a PC, choose Paste pairing pass. You can also expand Enter a pass manually and paste a pass or pairing link. The Android form supports the computer address, eight-digit pairing code, and security key when you have those separate details.

Manage paired phones

Press V to open the device picker. Use the arrows, press V again to move the focus, or click a row. Use Enter to select the highlighted device for sending or Screen Share.

Press M inside the picker to manage devices. Use R to rename the selected phone, X to forget it, or C to forget every paired phone. The two forget actions ask for confirmation with Y. A forgotten phone needs a fresh pairing.

On Android, Tools → Connect a PC → Forget paired computer removes the phone's saved computer connection. If a scan fails, expand Why won’t it connect? and follow the suggested checks in Troubleshooting.
