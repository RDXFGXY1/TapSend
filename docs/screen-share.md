# Screen Share

Screen Share shows a paired Android phone's screen in the Windows viewer. The Windows bundle includes the viewer files and requires the .NET 8 Runtime.

## Start a live session

1. On Windows, click **Screen Share** in the title bar or press **L**. Choose the phone if the picker opens.
2. On Android, open **Tools → Share screen to PC**.
3. Review and accept Android's capture prompt. Choose the app or screen scope offered by Android.

Press **Esc** in the viewer, press **L** again in TapSend, or tap **Stop screen sharing** on Android to end the session. The live stream is displayed without automatic recording. [Freeze & Grab](freeze-and-grab.md) lets you save a frame deliberately, and [Live Red Pen](live-red-pen.md) adds temporary annotations with the phone's permission.

If the stream stutters, check that both sides use matching current builds and that the phone can reach the computer over a stable local connection. Changing content produces new frames. The rate depends on the phone and connection.
