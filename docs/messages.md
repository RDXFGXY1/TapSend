# Messages and Scratchpad

Open **M** on Windows or **Tools → Messages** on Android to exchange short messages. The phone browser also has a **Messages** section.

Use **Scratchpad** for reusable text such as URLs, addresses, and commands.

1. On Windows, choose the phone with **V** and open Scratchpad with **K**.
2. Copy some text in Windows and press **P** inside Scratchpad to share it with the selected phone.
3. Use **↑/↓** and **Enter** to copy an item. Press **R** in Scratchpad to refresh.
4. On Android, open **Messages** and expand **Scratchpad**. The phone browser has its own Scratchpad section below Messages.

For screenshots or text shared to TapSend through Android's Share menu, use [Paste Stack](paste-stack.md).
