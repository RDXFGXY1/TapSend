Docs / Get started

Android app

The transfer home screen, Android Share, downloads, and nearby phones

Home and Tools

Computer connects to your paired PC and gives you actions to Send files and View shared files. Nearby phone lets you host or join a direct phone share. Tools opens computer pairing, Messages, and the screen-sharing options. Transfers and their recent results appear in the app as they happen.

Share from another app

  1. In Files, Photos, or another app, select a file, image, or text and tap Android's Share button.
  2. Choose TapSend in the Android share sheet.
  3. Review the selection in TapSend. Send files to a computer, or choose Add to Paste Stack for text and screenshots you want available on Windows later.

For computer uploads, the phone shows a result once the PC saves the file. If the PC has opened a temporary drop folder for your phone, you can confirm that destination or use the normal inbox.

Receive files

Open View shared files for regular downloads. When the PC offers files specifically to this phone, review the offer and accept or decline it. TapSend saves downloads under Downloads/TapSend. If the name is already in use, TapSend keeps the original and shows the name it used for the new copy.

Share directly between phones

  1. Put both phones on the same Wi-Fi network or hotspot.
  2. Choose Nearby phone on the sending device and add the files to a new share.
  3. On the receiving device, scan the displayed share QR or use its link. A phone browser can open it too.
  4. Keep TapSend open on the sending phone until the other device finishes. Approve any extra recipients if prompted, and stop the share when you're done.

Nearby shares use local HTTP. Anyone with the live link may be able to request access, so share it only with the intended recipients.
